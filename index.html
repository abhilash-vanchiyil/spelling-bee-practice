Microphone is blocked

Why there's no Microphone switch to find: this spelling page opens inside the Muse share page, and that outer page blocks the microphone before Chrome ever gets the request. That's why Chrome's permissions panel for this site lists only Sound — a Microphone option never appears for this page, and there is nothing in your Chrome settings to switch on. The keyboard trick below works instead.

To let Chrome use the microphone for this page:

  1. Tap the icon at the left end of Chrome's address bar (a lock, or a sliders/tune icon).
  2. Open Permissions / Site settings and set Microphone to Allow.
  3. Reload the page, tap the big microphone above, and tap Allow when Chrome asks.
  4. On a tablet, also check Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions and make sure Microphone is allowed for Chrome itself.

The fix that always works: tap “Type it instead,” tap the answer box, then tap the microphone key on the tablet's own keyboard and say the letters one by one, like “C — A — T.” The keyboard's microphone can't be blocked, and this app turns what it types into your spelling.
